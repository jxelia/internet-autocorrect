You’re all set

Internet Autocorrect quietly fixes slips in the text you read online, and marks every change. Reload any open tabs to start.

  • Wrong wordtheir → there, a apple → an apple
  • Misspellingdefinately → definitely
  • Typodont → don’t, the the → the

You stay in charge

Hover a marked word to see the original and undo it. Use the toolbar icon to turn the extension off for a site. Dialect, slang, regional spelling, names and other languages are left as written.

The optional AI checker

The rules work on their own. The AI checker also catches what they don’t know, using Chrome’s built-in model. It runs on this computer, can use its processor heavily, and needs a one-time download of a few gigabytes.

Your privacy

Page text stays on this computer. There are no accounts, analytics or ads.
